<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user wants to build a production-grade SaaS web application called **PropertyCheck AI**, an AI-powered property intelligence and verification platform for India.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
The platform aims to be an all-in-one solution for real estate due diligence, combining features from Landeed (land record discovery), Zillow (property intelligence), and Palantir (data integration). The vision is to create a fully automated engine for verifying property ownership, title history, legal risks, and market insights within minutes.

**Core Features:**
1.  **Intelligent Government Data Retrieval:** Use automated agents to scrape and collect data from various public land record systems.
2.  **Unified Property Data Graph:** Create a knowledge graph (e.g., using Neo4j) to connect properties, owners, transactions, and documents.
3.  **AI Document Processing:** An AI pipeline for OCR, classification, and entity extraction from property documents (Sale Deeds, EC, RTC, etc.).
4.  **Title Chain Reconstruction:** Automatically reconstruct and visualize historical ownership timelines to detect gaps or suspicious transfers.
5.  **AI Risk Score Engine:** The core feature, providing a 0-100 risk score based on ownership, document, and legal analysis. This logic is considered stable and should not be modified.
6.  **AI Legal Copilot:** A Gemini-powered assistant to analyze documents, highlight risks, and generate due diligence summaries for lawyers and home buyers.
7.  **Property Intelligence & Valuation:** Provide Zillow-style market data, including estimated value, price trends, and neighborhood insights.
8.  **Report Generation:** Create downloadable, shareable PDF reports summarizing all findings.
9.  **SaaS Dashboard & Enterprise APIs:** A modern UI for different user roles (Home Buyers, Lawyers, Banks) and a set of APIs for enterprise clients.

**Tech Stack:**
*   **Frontend:** Next.js (React + TypeScript), TailwindCSS + ShadCN UI, Mapbox/Leaflet.
*   **Backend:** Python, FastAPI, Modular/Microservices architecture.
*   **Databases:** PostgreSQL, Neo4j, Elasticsearch.
*   **AI/ML:** Google Gemini, custom models.
*   **Infrastructure:** Docker, Kubernetes, RabbitMQ/Redis, AWS/GCP.
